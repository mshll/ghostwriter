Fixes for suggestions in Chromium-based apps and while editing.

- **Suggestions stay visible.** In some apps the grey text could stop appearing after a while, even though Tab still accepted it.
- **Empty lines.** On a new, empty line the suggestion now shows at the cursor instead of on the field's first line.
- **Word accepts keep the rest.** After Tab takes a word, the rest of the suggestion stays.
- **Quiet while deleting.** Suggestions wait until you pause deleting.
- **Onboarding:** Tab moves between fields.

Requires macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon.
