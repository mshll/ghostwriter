Suggestions hold still while you pause, and setup lists the models already on your Mac.

- **Suggestions stay put.** Once a suggestion appears, it no longer changes into different words while you pause. It only grows.
- **Models already on your Mac in setup.** The model step lists base models found on your Mac, so you can use one instead of downloading.
- **Fixes:**
  - A suggestion that wraps onto the next line starts at the left edge of the text.

Requires macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon.
