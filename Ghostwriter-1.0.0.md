The first release of Ghostwriter: autocomplete for your whole Mac, on device.

- **Suggestions everywhere.** Grey text at your cursor in any app that macOS can read, also in the middle of a line. Tab takes the next word, the key above Tab takes all of it.
- **Many languages**, right to left too, with the multilingual Gemma 4 E2B model by default. Three other models are in Settings.
- **Sounds like you.** Learns your words and tone from what you write on this Mac, with instructions per app.
- **Reads the context:** the text near your cursor and text you copied recently.
- **Longer drafts** on demand.
- **Actions for selected text** and **one-shortcut proofreading** with Apple Intelligence.
- **Snippets**, **typo fixes** and **emoji**.
- **Private:** no cloud, no account, no telemetry. The only connections are model downloads from Hugging Face and update checks.

Requires macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon.
