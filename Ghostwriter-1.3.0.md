Your learned writing is now encrypted on disk, suggestions can draw on the window you just left, and ghost text sits where your caret is on new lines and at line ends.

- **Encrypted data.** The database with your recorded writing, snippets and statistics is encrypted with AES-256. The key is a random one in your login keychain, so there is nothing to set up. Your existing data is encrypted on the first launch of this version; earlier versions can no longer read it.
- **Context from the window you just left.** Read a page in your browser, switch to Notes, and suggestions can use what was on that page for the next 10 minutes. Nothing is stored. Turn it off in Settings > Context.
- **Google AI Mode.** Suggestions now show in Google's AI Mode prompt. Regular Google search stays off.
- **Ghost text in the right place.** Fixed suggestions drawn one line up on empty lines, at the end of the line above at a wrapped line's start, and slightly left of the text when a suggestion continues on the next line.
- **Fewer fields read.** Text from single-line fields in other parts of the window, such as card numbers and one-time codes, is no longer used as context.
- **Orca.** Suggestions read the Orca terminal you type in.

Requires macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon.
